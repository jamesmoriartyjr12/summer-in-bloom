# Bloom brand

Two sources. The [brand hub](https://bloom-brandhub.vercel.app) (updated June 2026) is the written system: strategy, logo, type, color, photography, layout, and the Bloom Object tool. The [website explorations](https://www.figma.com/design/HZwRfsXv0ufjEqpGZRsFjL/Bloom-Website-Explorations) are the website samples Marco is working from. When they disagree, name the conflict and leave it for James. Do not resolve it by restyling the site.

## Voice

Mission line: "Create great company."

Bloom is a venture-style partner led by proven operators. The voice is direct, confident, warm, and sharp. Direct means clear and compressed, not blunt. Confident means evidence and calm, not hype. Warm means a real person, not a brand voice on autopilot. Sharp means focused, not careless.

Values to sound like: relentless, human, fearless, strategic, creative.

## Color

Core palette from the explorations and the hub:

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#FAF6EC` | Light surface |
| Orange | `#FA4C1F` | Accent, links, orange logo |
| Lime | `#D8FF34` | Accent |
| Ink | `#070F18` in the explorations, `#070E18` on the hub | Dark surface and text |

Orange and lime match in both places. Ink differs by one digit. Do not invent a third value. Flag it.

The logo appears only in Ink, Paper, or Orange. Orange mark on light backgrounds. Paper mark on orange or ink backgrounds. Minimum digital width of the mark is 60px. Clearspace on every side is the height and width of the mark. Do not redraw, reweight, or re-space the wordmark.

## Type

- Headlines: Manifold Extended Heavy, all caps, tracking about -2%, line-height about 110–120%.
- Body and links: Archivo, line-height about 140%.
- Eyebrows, labels, metadata: JetBrains Mono.
- Do not mix weights or families inside one headline. Do not crash glyphs with tight tracking, and do not open tracking so far the word falls apart.

The site in this repo still sets display type in BIZ UDPMincho and body type in Helvetica Neue, on a chalk field (`#EBEBEB`) rather than Paper. That is known drift. A review reports whether a change moves toward this system. It does not restyle the page on its own.

## Image and motion

Photography is motion-blurred and long-exposure. Two families: nature (fields, trees, flowers) and urban (subways, traffic, streets). The Bloom Object tool turns a 3D object into a branded still or a turntable loop. Those exports are deliveries from Marco and Kayla, not something an agent generates and drops into `public/`.

## Layout

Margin is the longest side of the format divided by 25. The eyebrow is the entry point. Its width stays between one-sixth and one-third of the space inside the margins.
